# Imagens dos reinos (seleção de reino): prompts para o ChatGPT

Cada prompt gera um banner largo (1536 x 384, proporção 4:1) que representa o reino no card da tela "Escolha o reino". Anexe a imagem do Garen (ou de um cenário já pronto) só para o ChatGPT copiar o estilo do pixel. Nomes sugeridos: `reino-ixtal.png`, `reino-ionia.png`, `reino-demacia.png`, `reino-noxus.png`, `reino-shurima.png`, `reino-freljord.png`.

## Ixtal — reino-ixtal.png

```
Create a wide pixel-art banner illustration that represents the region "Ixtal" of Runeterra (League of Legends) for a game's realm-select card.

Pixel art, 32-bit era, clean hand-placed pixels, crisp hard edges, the same chunky pixel style as the attached reference. Absolutely NO anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO soft glow haze, NO smooth gradients (skies use a few flat color bands), NO text, NO letters, NO logos, NO frames, NO borders, NO UI, NO watermark, no characters in the foreground.

FORMAT: image size 1536 x 384 px (a wide 4:1 banner). The scene is a panoramic landscape seen from the side (like a game title-screen strip): sky and distant landmarks in the upper two thirds, ground and foreground details at the bottom. The most important landmark sits slightly right of center, and the left and right edges are calm and simple so the image can be cropped a little on the sides. Keep strong, readable silhouettes that still work when the banner is shrunk to about 176 x 44 px.

SCENE: a dense ancient jungle of Ixtal at golden hour: a giant stepped stone temple pyramid covered in vines and glowing jade runes rising above the canopy, huge twisted jungle trees with hanging moss, a jade-green feathered serpent silhouette coiling around the temple, hanging orchids with pink flowers, ray-like shafts of warm light between the trees, a calm stone river in the foreground with a few mossy stones.

PALETTE: deep jungle greens #2F6B2E and #285C28, moss teal #2E7F66, jade #3AC79A, warm gold light #FFD27A, a little pink #E87AA8 for orchids, dark brown trunks #3A2A1A.

The whole image is fully painted edge to edge, no transparent areas, no green screen.
```
